### v0.2.3 ###
- 2026-09-18 - Add the _Event custom type for he elements of
    RankingSession.history.
- 2026-09-18 - Separate _oneRound into _oneRound, _resetRound, and
    _transferLastToNextRound.
- 2026-09-18 - Append events to RankingSession.history. (session start,
    new round, face-off result, transfer last to next round, addition to
    ranking, bringing back those eliminated by newly ranked, session end)
- 2026-09-18 - Add printHistory() and _faceOffToString() to Ranking Session to

### v0.2.2 ###
- 2026-09-18 - Separate RankingSession._checkIfOneLeft into 3 functions
    with one purpose each. (_putNextFavoriteInRank, _bringBackEliminated,
    _allBettersAreRanked)

### v0.2.1 ###
- 2026-09-18 - If no contender is chosen in a face-off, they all advance
    to next round.
- 2026-09-18 - Added errors for when adding a better/lesser to a Rankable
    that already has it in its betters/lessers.
- 2026-09-18 - Add condition in Ranking.RankingSession._checkIfOneLeft() to
    only bring back an eliminated Rankable if all its betters are in the
    ranking.
